## Central idea
A gate voltage controls charge near the semiconductor surface through an insulating dielectric. In an enhancement-mode device, a sufficiently large gate bias creates a conducting channel between source and drain. The presentation uses this physical picture to introduce threshold voltage and cutoff, linear and saturation behavior.

Its scaling discussion addresses the motivation to increase integration density and improve performance, followed by challenges such as leakage, heat and reduced electrostatic control in short channels.

## Reading notes
The deck is introductory coursework. Some slides use simplified explanations and numerical examples. In particular, process-node names should not automatically be read as physical gate lengths, and statements about oxide electric fields, atomic dimensions and lithographic resolution require care. The original slide wording is preserved in the supplied presentation.
