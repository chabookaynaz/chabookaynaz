# Electrical Steel: Processing and Applications

**English reading guide**  
Group academic poster by Rasul Alitabar Malekshah, Rasol Motamed and Aynaz Chabouk (names transliterated from the poster)

## Overview
The original poster is already in English. It compares grain-oriented (GO) and non-grain-oriented (NGO) electrical steels and connects their processing routes with electromagnetic applications.

## Main topics
- The poster distinguishes preferential grain orientation in GO steel from the more broadly distributed orientations of NGO steel.
- Its manufacturing discussion covers melting, slab casting, hot rolling, cold rolling, annealing and coating.
- The GO route includes additional annealing and texture-development steps, with Goss texture identified as important for transformer applications.
- The application discussion relates electrical steels to motors, generators, transformer cores and related electromagnetic components.

## How to read it
Begin with the manufacturing section, compare the GO and NGO routes, and then connect those routes to the application illustrations. Processing temperatures, compositions and thickness ranges should be understood as values presented in the coursework poster rather than universal specifications for every electrical-steel grade.
