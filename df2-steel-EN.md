## Project overview
This laboratory project examined the microstructure of DF2 steel after austempering at 320 °C. The shared experiment included six holding times; this report focuses on the sample held for 15 minutes. The report was submitted on 6 January 2026.

## Experimental procedure
1. Austenitize six DF2 steel specimens at 900 °C for 20 minutes.
2. Transfer the specimens to a salt bath at 320 °C.
3. Hold individual specimens for 2, 5, 7, 15, 30 and 60 minutes, respectively.
4. Cool in air after the isothermal hold.
5. Perform Rockwell C hardness testing and prepare specimens for metallographic examination.

The report describes this experimental sequence but does not provide a numerical hardness table or a quantitative comparison of all six specimens.

## Heat-treatment principle
Austempering involves rapid cooling from the austenitic region to an intermediate temperature, followed by an isothermal hold intended to promote bainitic transformation. The background section explains the route using a time-temperature-transformation diagram and discusses reduced distortion and a favorable combination of strength and toughness as motivations for this treatment. These are background considerations rather than properties measured in this report.

## Observations and interpretation
The authors interpret the 15-minute specimen as predominantly martensitic, with dispersed bainitic regions. The report describes needle-like and plate-like features and identifies the bainite as lower bainite, consistent with the selected holding temperature.

The proposed explanation is that some austenite transformed to bainite during the isothermal hold, while remaining austenite transformed to martensite during final cooling. On this interpretation, the 15-minute hold did not complete the bainitic transformation.

These phase assignments are the original authors' interpretation of optical micrographs; phase fractions and independent phase identification are not reported. The discussion also suggests reduced internal stresses, but no residual-stress measurements are supplied.
